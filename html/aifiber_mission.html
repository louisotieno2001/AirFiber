Consider this

Does a young child growing up in a village have the same opportunities for success as others raised in one of Nairobi's suburbs?

William Kamkwamba (Masitala) learnt how to build a wind turbine by reading old books. He powered his home, brought drinking water to his village.

He proved it's possible.

Mourine Apuu (Lodwar) got an opportunity to learn IT at Learning Lions. She went on to become a teacher, bringing Media and IT skills to her community.

She proved it's possible.

Julius Yego (Cheptonon) learnt to throw a Javelin by watching YouTube videos. He won silver at the Olympics, and holds the African record.

He proved it's possible.

That's the power of knowledge and connections made possible.
